Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

When you create an account or make a booking, we collect your name, email, phone number, and booking details. We use secure third-party providers to process payments and never store full card numbers ourselves.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process bookings, communicate with you about your trip, provide support, and — with your consent — send you occasional offers relevant to Sharm El-Sheikh travel.

Sharing With Partners

We share only the booking details necessary (name, contact info, dates) with the specific hotel, excursion operator, transfer company or restaurant you book with, so they can fulfil your reservation.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data, including encrypted connections and secure authentication.

Your Rights

You can review, update or request deletion of your personal data at any time from your account settings, or by contacting our support team.
