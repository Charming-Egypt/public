# 2026-11-04 — dedicated Reviews page (voucher-only)

New page **/reviews/** (www + app): read guest reviews, and write one.

## Reading
- Summary card (average, count, 1–5★ split), filters (type / with photos / newest · top rated), review cards with verified-voucher badge, photos (lightbox), host reply, "show more".
- Names are shown as "First L." only. If there are no real reviews yet, the bundled testimonials (`shared/data/reviews.json`) are shown.

## Writing (rules are enforced in the database, the page only mirrors them)
- Needs a **voucher number** (= booking id, `DS-XXXXXX`) **and** the signed-in account that owns it. Signed-out visitors see a sign-in prompt.
- **Nothing about the guest is typed**: no name, no nationality, no e-mail. Name + avatar come from the account (`profiles`). The form accepts only star rating, comment (10–1000 chars) and up to 4 photos.
- The voucher is checked before the form opens (`lookup_review_voucher`) and again on submit (`submit_review`): it must belong to the account, be confirmed/paid, not be reviewed yet, and the service date must have passed. Someone else's voucher answers exactly like a non-existent one.
- Photos are resized in the browser, uploaded to the new `review-media` bucket under `<uid>/…`, and `submit_review` only accepts URLs from that folder.
- "Ready to review" shortcuts list the account's own eligible vouchers (tap = fills the voucher number).
