Why Sharm El Sheikh Is a Paradise

Unlike destinations requiring long offshore journeys, Sharm El Sheikh is fringed by living reefs right off its beaches. Calm bays shelter shallow lagoons for families, while offshore reefs plunge into deep cobalt walls.

Expect clouds of anthias, stingrays, butterflyfish, and frequent green sea turtles. Choosing the right site for your comfort level ensures the best experience.

Top 5 Snorkeling Spots in Detail

01 · Ras MohammedAdvanced

Egypt’s premier marine reserve featuring towering vertical coral walls, deep drop-offs, and abundant pelagic life. Best suited for confident swimmers due to outer currents.

02 · Naama BayBeginners

Sheltered natural crescent shoreline blocking strong waves. Wade directly off soft sand into shallow turquoise water with coral bommies—ideal for kids and first-timers.

03 · Tiran IslandAll Levels

Accessible by boat, Jackson and Gordon Reefs boast some of the healthiest hard and soft corals in the northern Red Sea with frequent turtle sightings.

04 · Ras Um SidAll Levels

Tucked beneath Hadaba cliffs, delivering dramatic underwater scenery without a boat. A short swim brings you to a breathtaking forest of sea fans.

05 · Shark’s BayFamilies

A peaceful, protected cove prized for effortless sandy walk-in channels. Glide over vibrant staghorn coral, anemones, and graceful blue-spotted rays.

5 Golden Rules for Red Sea Snorkeling

🌅
Hit the water before 10 AM: Morning light brings out vivid reef colors before afternoon chop.
🪸
Never touch or stand: Coral polyps break instantly. Use designated jetties.
👕
Wear a UV rash guard: Skip chemical sunscreens that harm coral.
🧭
Check beach flags: Swim into the current first so returning is effortless.
🤿
Test your mask seal: Inhale through your nose on dry land to check for leaks.
